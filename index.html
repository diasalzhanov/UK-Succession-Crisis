How this was measured

Data: Companies House Basic Company Data and the People with Significant Control register (owners' month and year of birth), snapshot November 2026.

  • Owner-managed: at least one individual owner and no company owner.
  • Lead owner: the individual with the largest shareholding; ties go to the older owner.
  • Excluded: dormant, non-trading and subsidiary companies, companies under 2 years old, and property-holding companies.

Limitations

  • Region is based on the registered office postcode, which is sometimes an accountant's address, so London may look larger and rural regions smaller.
  • An owner aged 65+ may already have a successor planned. This measures exposure, not certain closures.
  • Sole traders and partnerships are not on the register.
